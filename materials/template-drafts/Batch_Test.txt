### Data Entry Rules

* One row represents one measurement.
* `Time` = elapsed experimental time.
* `Concentration` = measured concentration at that time.
* `pH` = measured pH at that time.
* Do not put units inside individual cells.
* Use `NA` when a parameter is not applicable.
* Use `ND` when the concentration is below the detection limit.
