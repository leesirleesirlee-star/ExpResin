### Data Entry Rules

* One row represents one measurement/time point.
* `Time` = elapsed experimental time.
* `Potential / Voltage` = measured electrochemical potential or cell voltage, depending on the cell configuration.
* `Current` = measured current.
* `Concentration` = measured concentration of the target substance.
* `pH` = measured pH.
* Concentration and pH may be left as `NA` at time points where they were not measured.
* Do not manually calculate derived quantities in the raw-data table.
